# Ask or decide

## In
- The question, from the work that raised it.
- What the owner has already said or decided, and the work's own inputs. Check only the sources that exist.

## Out
- What the sources settle, and marked defaults, written into the work where its skill says to write.
- Questions for the owner, each with your pick, returned to the work that raised them.

## Think
- Does anything the owner has said or decided, or the work's inputs, settle it?
- Is it hard to undo, and does it change what comes next?

## Act
1. Check what the owner has said or decided, and the work's inputs. Write in what they settle.
2. Ask the owner only what's both hard to undo and changes what comes next, each with your pick.
3. For everything else, take a default and mark it.
