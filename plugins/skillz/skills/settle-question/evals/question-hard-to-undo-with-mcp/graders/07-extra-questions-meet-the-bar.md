Every other question the result raises beside its answer names, in the result's own words, what the work cannot do until that question is answered, and none is returned that the work can go on without. A new question about a setting, an option, a notice, an edge case or a later choice that the work could be finished without (for example whether the default should be configurable, whether the output should say it was cut short, what a limit of 0 means, whether finished items are hidden, whether amounts may be shown in another currency) fails. The asked question itself, returned as a default or a question, is not an extra. A result that raises no other question passes.
