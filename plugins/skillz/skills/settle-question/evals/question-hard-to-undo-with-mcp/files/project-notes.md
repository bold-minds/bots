# Ledgerleaf (invoicing app for freelancers), launch notes
- Invoices are created in the app and paid by card through a Stripe account.
- The Stripe account is created and in test mode. Its settlement currency and business country have not been set.
- Customers so far: none. The first paying customers are planned for next month.
- Invoice templates and the app's display currency are already built to take any ISO currency code.
- Nothing in these notes or from anyone says which currency the account should settle in.
