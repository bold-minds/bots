# Ask or decide

## In
- The question: what must be chosen, and what the work can't do until it is.
- What people have already said or decided that bears on the question, in their words, and the work's own inputs. Either may be empty; use what there is.

## Out
- What the sources settle, and marked defaults, written into the work where its skill says to write.
- Questions for the caller to decide, each with your pick, returned to the caller.
- The Think answers, one line per question, kept apart from the work, returned to the caller.

## Think
- Does anything people have said or decided, or the work's inputs, settle it?
- Is it hard to undo, and does it change what comes next?

## Act
1. Check the question against `## In`: it says what must be chosen and what the work can't do until it is. If it doesn't, return it to the caller, saying exactly what's missing.
2. Check what people have said or decided, and the work's inputs. Write in what they settle.
3. Return to the caller only what's both hard to undo and changes what comes next, each with your pick.
4. For everything else, take a default and mark it: end its line in the work with ` *`, and list it under the work's defaults taken.
5. Return the Think answers, one line per question, apart from the work, to the caller.
