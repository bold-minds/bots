# sprout requirements

## Plants
**SP1. Someone can see every plant, by name.**
**SP2. Someone can open one plant and see its name.**
**SP6. Someone can rename a plant; the new name is kept.**
**SP7. A plant's name is 1 to 40 characters once spaces at the ends are trimmed.**
**SP8. Renaming a plant that no longer exists says "That plant is gone".**
