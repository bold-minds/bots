The revised design adds no package, function, route, library, PR or test that is not in the earlier design or asked for by the markup. A change to a line's estimate that the markup's change forces does not count as added. A new entry under `Defaults taken:`, a standard-library function named to settle a detail the markup left open (for example how length is counted), and any new line the markup doesn't touch and no change forces, all count as added (fix c).
