Apart from the design, the returned message gives a change list: each line of the design that was added or changed, what it adds, drops or rewords against the earlier line, and the source behind each (markup note 1 or 2, or the change that forces it). Every line that differs from the earlier design appears in it, and no item cites only "implied" or "wiring" as its source.
