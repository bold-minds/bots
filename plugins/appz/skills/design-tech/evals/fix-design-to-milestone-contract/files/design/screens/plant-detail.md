# Plant page (GET /plants/{id})

- The plant's name as the heading.
- Under it, "Last watered: <when>" or "Not watered yet".
- A "Watered it" button. After it is pressed, the line above changes to "Last watered: just now" without leaving the page.
- When a watering can't be saved, "Couldn't save that, try again" shows under the button, and the line above stays as it was.
