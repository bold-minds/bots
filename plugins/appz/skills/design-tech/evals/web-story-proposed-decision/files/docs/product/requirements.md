# sprout requirements

## Plants
**SP1. Someone can see every plant, by name.**
**SP2. Someone can open one plant and see its name.**

## Watering
**SP3. Someone can log that they watered a plant; the time is kept.**
**SP4. A plant's page shows when it was last watered, or "Not watered yet".**
**SP5. Logging a watering for a plant that no longer exists says "That plant is gone".**
**SP6. When the app can't save to its database, logging a watering says "Couldn't save that, try again", and the plant's page still shows the last watering it had.**
