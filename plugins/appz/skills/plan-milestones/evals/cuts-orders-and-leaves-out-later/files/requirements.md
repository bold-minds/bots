# Requirements: Toolshed (a tool-lending site for one neighborhood)

Lines marked ` *` were added by AI; the rest are in the words people used.

- R1: People start at the Home page, which links to the Catalog, My Loans and, for stewards, the Steward console.
- R2: Members browse the Catalog and open an Item page for any tool.
- R3: A member borrows a tool from its Item page by choosing a return date at Checkout.
- R4: A member sees My Loans with every tool they hold and its due date.
- R5: A member returns a tool and the loan closes. *
- R6: A steward adds a new tool to the Catalog from the Steward console.
- R7: A tool marked "in repair" shows "In repair" in the Catalog and cannot be borrowed at Checkout.
- R8: A steward marks a tool "in repair" or "back in service" from the Steward console.
- R9: Members get a reminder two days before a tool is due.
- R10: Every page works with only a keyboard.
- R11: Members pay a late fee when a tool comes back after its due date.
- R12: A steward sees a list of overdue loans in the Steward console.
