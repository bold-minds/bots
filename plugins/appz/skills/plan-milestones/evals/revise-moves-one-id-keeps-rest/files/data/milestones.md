## Milestone 1: Look through what we have
After it, people can: start at the Home page, browse the Catalog and open any tool's Item page, all from the keyboard.
Requirements: R1, R2, R10

## Milestone 2: Borrow a tool
After it, members can: borrow a tool at Checkout, see it in My Loans with its due date, and return it.
Requirements: R3, R4, R5

## Milestone 3: Stewards look after the tools
After it, stewards can: add a tool, mark a tool in repair or back in service, and see a list of overdue loans in the Steward console.
Requirements: R6, R7, R8, R12

## Milestone 4: A nudge before it's due
After it, members can: get a reminder two days before a tool is due.
Requirements: R9
