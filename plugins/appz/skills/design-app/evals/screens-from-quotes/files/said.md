# What people said about Lobby Shelf

Everything below is what people said, in their words, in the order they said it. Nothing here was added by AI.
Source: the building's kickoff chat, by message number.

- (msg 1, organizer) "Our building has a shelf of books in the lobby and nobody knows what's on it. I want an app on our phones that lists what's there."
- (msg 2, organizer) "Anyone in the building can add a book: title and author, and a photo of the cover if they want."
- (msg 3, resident on floor 4) "When I take a book I want to mark it as borrowed so others don't go looking for it."
- (msg 4, resident on floor 4) "And mark it back when I return it."
- (msg 5, organizer) "Let people search by title or author. We have maybe 200 books."
- (msg 6, organizer) "Borrow for three weeks, then it should nudge you to bring it back."
- (msg 7, resident on floor 2) "Most of us will use it on our phones, but I'd add books from my laptop."
- (msg 8, organizer) "No accounts with passwords, please. A link we share in the building chat is enough."
- (msg 9, resident on floor 2) "Show who has a book? Maybe just a first name."
