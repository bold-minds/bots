# What people said about ToolShelf

ToolShelf is a small app for neighbors on one street to lend tools to each other.
Everything under "People said" is in their own words, in the order said. Everything under "AI notes" is not from a person.

## People said

- Mara, Nov 3, 9:05am: "I want to list a tool I own with a photo and a name, that's it."
- Mara, Nov 3, 9:12am: "Neighbors should be able to ask to borrow it for a few days."
- Dev, Nov 3, 9:20am: "I need to see who has my drill right now without asking around."
- Mara, Nov 3, 2:40pm: "If someone is late bringing it back I want a nudge sent for me, I hate being the nag."
- Dev, Nov 4, 8:15am: "Only people on my street should see my stuff."
- Dev, Nov 4, 8:30am: "I don't want a points or rating thing. Just lend and borrow."
- Mara, Nov 5, 11:00am: "When I get a request I want to say yes or no from my phone."
- Mara, Nov 5, 1:15pm: "I want the Ask to borrow button to be big and green so my dad can find it."
- Dev, Nov 5, 1:30pm: "Later on, I'd like neighbors to be able to see a tool's history of past loans."
- Mara, Nov 5, 1:45pm: "Just an idea: maybe a map of the street someday."
