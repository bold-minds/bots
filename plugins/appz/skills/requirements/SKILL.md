# Requirements

## Think
- Where did the owner say this?
- If they didn't, would they be surprised it's missing?

## Act
- Include what the owner said, what's needed for it to work, and what they'd plainly expect.
- `**A1. <one plain sentence in the owner's terms>.**` then `Owner: "<shortest quote>" (<date, time>)`
- End inferred lines with ` *`. List their reasons at the bottom.
- Group by area, in the owner's words. Put tests in the stories.
